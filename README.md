# FarmerML — Farmer Income Prediction & Credit Risk Modelling

**Aurelius Consulting & Analytics Club · Recruitment 2026 · Machine Learning Track (AgriIntel)**

Predicts farmer income from demography, land holdings, local climate and living-index indicators, then uses the model to recommend interventions for low-income farmers and to quantify seasonal credit risk.

**Metric:** MAPE (lower is better) · **Target:** `log(1 + income)`, exponentiated back for evaluation and submission.

---

## Results

| Model | Train MAPE | Validation MAPE | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Linear Regression (StandardScaler pipeline), baseline | 0.2742 | 0.2698 | -0.0043 |
| **XGBoost (Optuna-tuned), final** | **0.1402** | **0.2049** | 0.0648 |

Validation is a 20% hold-out (`random_state=42`). XGBoost cuts validation MAPE by about 6.5 points over the baseline. The train/validation gap is modest, so overfitting is controlled.

## Pipeline overview

### Phase 1 — EDA
- Income is heavily right-skewed, so the model predicts `log1p(income)`.
- Land is long-tailed, and log(land) vs log(income) is far more linear.
- Mandi proximity is bimodal, which motivates an inverse market-access score.
- Village Agri Category (Poor / Average / Good) has a monotone income gradient, so it is encoded as an ordinal.
- Kharif rainfall varies widely across farmers, which motivates volatility and deviation features.
- Correlation heatmap (top 15 predictors), scatter plots, regional/categorical breakdowns and seasonal rainfall boxplots are included.

### Phase 2 — Data preparation & feature engineering

**Missing values**
- Columns with more than 70% missing are dropped.
- Features with 10–30% missing use a KNN imputer (k=5).
- Other numerics use the mean if |skew| < 1, otherwise the median.
- Categoricals use the mode.
- Imputation is re-run after feature engineering.

**Outliers**
- Values are capped at the 3×IQR fence, applied before and after feature engineering.

**Encoding**

| Feature | Method |
|---|---|
| Village Agri Category | Ordinal (0/1/2) |
| SEX, MARITAL_STATUS | Binary flags (`is_male`, `is_married`) |
| REGION and other low-cardinality categoricals (≤8 unique) | One-hot |
| State, DISTRICT, VILLAGE | Dropped (high cardinality, noise risk) |

**Engineered features**
- `land_log`, `log_non_agri`, `land_per_income_proxy`
- `market_access_score` = 1 / (1 + mandi_km), and `land_market_interact`
- `rainfall_mean`, `rainfall_volatility`, `rainfall_cv`
- `rainfall_dev_k22`, `rainfall_dev_r22` (deviation from the farmer's own average)
- `kharif_yoy_delta`, `rabi_yoy_delta`
- `drought_season_count`, `kharif_rabi_ratio`, `income_volatility_score`
- Per-season temperature range (`Tmax − Tmin`)

### Phase 3 — Modelling
- **Baseline:** StandardScaler → LinearRegression.
- **Advanced:** XGBoost tuned with Optuna (30 trials, 5-fold CV, minimising MAPE).
- **Search space:**
  - `n_estimators` 300–800
  - `learning_rate` 0.01–0.08 (log)
  - `max_depth` 4–8
  - `subsample` 0.7–1.0
  - `colsample_bytree` 0.6–1.0
  - `min_child_weight` 1–6
  - `reg_alpha` 0–3
  - `reg_lambda` 1–8
- The final model is refit on the full training data before test inference.

### Bonus A — Intervention recommender
- SHAP TreeExplainer on the trained model.
- Bottom 25% of farmers by predicted income are isolated.
- Fixed variables (rainfall stats, deviations, drought count, Kharif/Rabi ratio) are excluded.
- The remaining features are ranked by mean |SHAP| within the bottom quartile, and the top 2 are the actionable levers.
- Counterfactual: each lever is shifted to its 75th percentile, and the average % income gain is reported.

### Bonus B — Seasonal volatility modelling
- K022 / R022 / K021 / R021 / R020 are reshaped into a farmer × season × year panel, not flattened.
- Per-farmer rainfall mean, std and coefficient of variation are computed.
- Volatility is compared against log(income) and income quartile.
- Kharif/Rabi imbalance is measured as |kharif_share − 0.5|.
- Drought frequency (share of seasons below P30 rainfall) is correlated with log(income), with r and p-value reported.
- Takeaway: volatile and drought-prone farmers are a riskier credit bet regardless of average income.
